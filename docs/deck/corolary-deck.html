The problem

DeFi charges everyone the same price for being a stranger

Aave and Compound require 150 to 200 percent collateral from every borrower, without exception. A wallet that has repaid 40 loans over two years is priced exactly like a wallet created five minutes ago.

Capital sits idle
Every extra dollar of collateral is capital that is not lent, not earning, and not doing anything except sitting as excess security.
Reputation has no value
A perfect repayment record cannot travel. It is locked inside Ethereum contract storage, and it cannot be proven anywhere else, including on the chain built around credit.
02
Why this is still open

On-chain credit scoring is five years old and still has no economic traction

  • They sell a number you have to trust. A score computed off-chain, delivered as an assertion, with no way for a lender to check the evidence underneath it.
  • The ones that reached a market reached the wrong one. Undercollateralized lending to anonymous wallets with no recourse. That product has been tried, and the losses are on record.
Corolary changes the output, not just the model.
Every loan stays fully over-collateralized. What a proven borrower gets is a smaller number, not a waived one. That single decision is what separates this from the category behind it.
03
The solution
Corolary proves real Ethereum mainnet lending history on Creditcoin, and turns it into a price. Required collateral falls from 150 percent to 110 percent.

Not uncollateralized lending. Proven borrowers simply lock far less capital, and every number can be traced back to a real mainnet transaction.

04
How it works

Three layers on one proof

Layer 1
FactRegistry
Verifies an Ethereum transaction through the Attestcoin precompile, decodes the receipt, and stores the fact permanently. Paid once, readable forever by any Creditcoin dApp.
→
Layer 2
CreditGraph
Computes a score from 0 to 1000 entirely on-chain. Every component points at the specific facts behind it, so a lender can audit the number instead of trusting it.
→
Layer 3
EfficiencyMarket
Reads the score and prices collateral against it. The ratio is locked at borrow time, so a later score drop cannot make a healthy position liquidatable.

An indexer watches Aave V3, Morpho Blue, SparkLend and Compound V3 on Ethereum mainnet, waits for attestor consensus, buys proofs in batches, and submits them to Creditcoin.

05
Attestcoin integration

Attestcoin is not a feature here. It is the floor

Mainnet onlyFactRegistry accepts chainKey 3, Ethereum mainnet, and nothing else. Sepolia returns false by contract policy, so nobody can farm a score with tokens they minted themselves.
Inclusion is not successThe Block Prover proves a transaction was included in a mined block, not that it succeeded. Every path decodes the receipt and requires receiptStatus 1 before recording anything.
Emitter validationProving a log exists is not proving it came from Aave. The decoder is selected by the emitting address, so a copycat contract emitting fake events has no valid path in.
Eager provingA proof built inside the cheap window costs roughly 10x less. We prove events while they are fresh and store the fact permanently, never on demand in a hot path.
Batched verificationOne call amortizes cost across up to 10 transactions within a 999 block span. The batch is all or nothing, so a corrupt member is split out rather than retried.

08
Why it resists gaming

Faking a history costs the same as having one

Mainnet gas is the toll
Every fact behind a score is a real Ethereum transaction that someone already paid for. There is no cheap chain to manufacture a record on, because chainKey 3 is the only one accepted.
Time cannot be bought
History duration is measured from the first proven fact. A wallet created today cannot buy its way to a two year record at any price.
Self dealing earns nothing
Compound base asset flows are deliberately not mapped, because supplying and repaying are indistinguishable there. Only unambiguous events count.

Every check lives in the contract rather than in an off-chain policy, so the rules are readable by the same people who are subject to them.

Business model

Five streams on one set of facts

Origination fee0.5 percent of principal, taken once at drawdown. A 10,000 dollar loan pays 50 dollars.
Interest spread1 to 2 percent between what borrowers pay and what suppliers receive. Recurring while the position is open.
Credit Graph APIProtocols that want to read Corolary scores for their own underwriting pay for access beyond the free tier. Recurring, not a one time purchase.
Sponsored factsA lending protocol that wants its events indexed and proven can fund the integration and the ongoing proving cost, and reach an already verified borrower base.
B2B licensingCredal serves off-chain credit data to fintech lenders. Corolary supplies the crypto-native half it has never had.

12
Strategic fit

Corolary is not a competitor to Credal. It is the missing half

What Credal already knows
Who is creditworthy from off-chain financial history. Fintech loans, instalment payments, KYC. 4.27 million credit transactions, 79.7 million dollars in volume, 337,000 customers.
What Corolary adds
Who is creditworthy from on-chain financial history. DeFi borrowing and repayment, proven cryptographically, with no trusted third party data in the path.

Creditcoin was built by Gluwa around credit for emerging markets, and all of that traction is off-chain. The score layer drawn from crypto-native activity is the one piece the ecosystem does not have yet.
